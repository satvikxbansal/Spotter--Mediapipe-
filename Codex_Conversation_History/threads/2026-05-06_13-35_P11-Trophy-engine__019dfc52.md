Phase 11 — Robust trophy engine
Why this prompt changed

The trophy system should be motivational and honest. Some trophies in the design imply data the app does not currently have:

Neon Pulse says 150+ BPM, but the app currently has face/exertion proxy signals, not heart-rate data. The current live trainer has optional face/exertion analysis, not a heart-rate sensor integration.
Heavy Metal says 10,000 KG Total Volume, but the app does not yet collect dumbbell weight/load.
Burpee Beast should stay locked/coming-soon unless burpees are supported and validated.

So the trophy engine should support coming soon / unavailable metric trophies instead of faking progress.
